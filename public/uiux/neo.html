A bank you can just ask.

A concept for the next neo by Bank Audi. Dark by default, it opens straight to your money, and you run it by asking: "send Rami $20", "find an ATM with dollars". It fixes what users report most: a frozen logo screen, maintenance at the worst time, top-ups that seem to vanish, and no access from a laptop.

    Every screen works. Try the green pill. FIX marks a feature built from a real user review.

    Unofficial design concept by G.VIISUALS. Names, amounts, ATM availability and references are sample data. Not an official neo or Bank Audi product.
